What changes in a standard 8-player game?

The simplified 5-player game omits some standard rules. This game has 5 Townsfolk, 1 Outsider, 1 Minion, and 1 Demon.

Outsiders are good, but their abilities often hinder the good team. Think of them as troublesome teammates.

First night · Good information

Seat 1 · Chef Poisoned

Learns the number 0. Seats 7 and 8 are actually adjacent, so the correct answer is 1.

Seat 2 · Empath

Both living neighbours are good, so the result is 0.

Seat 3 · Fortune Teller

Checks Seats 7 and 8 and receives yes. At least one of them is a Demon.

Seat 6 · “Investigator” Actually the Drunk

Is told that one of Seats 5 and 7 is the Poisoner. They do not know the information may be true or false.

The Fortune Teller may also be misled by a good Red Herring who registers as a Demon.

☀

Day one

After discussion, Seat 7 becomes the main suspect.

Seat 3 nominates Seat 7.
Seat 7 receives 4 votes, and nobody later ties or exceeds that total.
At the end of the day, Seat 7 is executed.
The Demon is still alive. The game continues.
☾

Second night

Seat 8 · Imp

Chooses to kill Seat 3 · Fortune Teller.

Seat 4 · Undertaker

Learns that Seat 7, executed during the day, was the Poisoner.

☀

Day two

Seat 2 nominates the Virgin at Seat 5.
The Virgin ability triggers, and the good player at Seat 2 is executed immediately.
☾

Third night

Seat 8 · Imp

Kills Seat 4 · Undertaker. The Undertaker dies before waking and receives no information that night.

♟

Day three

Four players remain alive: 3 good players and 1 Demon.
If the group executes the wrong player, the Demon wins after killing at night.
If nobody is executed, the Demon kills at night and good gets one final chance on day four.
